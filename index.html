Questions before we build?

Clear answers. Practical next steps.

Do you work with NRIs and overseas business owners?

Yes. We can plan and deliver suitable projects remotely, with scope, milestones, communication and reporting agreed up front.

Can you build a CRM around our existing sales process?

Yes. We start by mapping your lead stages, handoffs, reporting needs and existing tools, then scope the smallest useful system before implementation.

Can you guarantee Google rankings or a fixed number of leads?

No. Search rankings and campaign performance depend on competition, budget, offer, audience and other factors. We focus on sound implementation, clear measurement and continuous improvement—not guaranteed outcomes.

Do you provide fully autonomous AI agents?

It depends on the task and integrations. We distinguish working integrations from prototypes and keep human approval for high-impact actions such as publishing, spending or contacting customers when appropriate.
